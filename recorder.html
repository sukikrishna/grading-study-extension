Study screen recorder

Keep this pinned tab open while you grade. One video covers the whole session (breaks pause it). When the session ends, the video and the log files are saved to Downloads/grading-study/ and the temporary pieces kept in the browser are deleted. Nothing is uploaded.

Not recording

Click Start recording, choose Entire screen, then Share. You are taken back to Gradescope automatically. Use the study bar at the top of the Gradescope page to pause and to end the session.
