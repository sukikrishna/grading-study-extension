Grading study: how it works

Thank you for taking part. This takes about two minutes to read.

What the study is about

Each answer on Gradescope already has AI-generated feedback under Autograder Results. Your job is the usual one: check the student's work, apply the rubric, and add comments, using the AI feedback however you find useful. The study measures how long grading takes and how graders use the AI feedback. A study bar on the grading page does almost all of the record-keeping.

Each session

  1. Open any Gradescope grading page. In the study bar, paste your grader ID (from the study team) and click Start session. You only need to paste it the first time; it's remembered after that, and shown on the bar as "Grader 1a2b3c4d".
  2. A recorder tab opens. Click Start recording, choose Entire screen, and click Share. You're returned to Gradescope.
  3. Grade as usual. Timing is automatic: it starts when you open a student's answer to a question and stops when you move on or return to the question list. For every answer: apply the rubric, add comments, then press one AI use button. Then click Next or Next Ungraded.
  4. Walking away? Press ⏸ Pause, then Resume grading when you're back.
  5. Done for now? Press ■ End twice. Your files are saved to Downloads/grading-study/. Upload that folder to the study drive.

You can't move to another answer until you've pressed an AI-use button or Skip. If you leave an answer some other way, the bar asks about it on the next page.

Privacy

  • Recordings and logs stay on your computer until you upload them to the study drive. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically.
  • Close unrelated windows and personal tabs before sharing your screen.
  • Pause whenever you step away. The recording stops, and the student work on screen is hidden.

You can reopen this page any time with the ? button on the study bar. Don't see the bar? Reload the Gradescope tab.
